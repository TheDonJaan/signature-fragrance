GOOD TO KNOW

What exactly is a decant?

A decant is authentic fragrance transferred from its original bottle into a smaller spray atomizer. It is the same fragrance liquid in a different container.

Are the fragrances authentic?

Authenticity is the operating standard for the business model: decants are filled from genuine original bottles. Signature Fragrance is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the fragrance houses shown.

Which size should I choose?

Start with 2 mL if you are unsure, 5 mL if you want it in rotation, and 10 mL when you already know you like the scent. Approximate spray counts are shown in the size guide above.

How do Signature Sets work?

Choose 2, 5 or 10 mL first. You will only see the two sets designed for that size. Most sets are one-time purchases; the 5 mL Signature Rotation is the only monthly option in this preview.

Why is Canada-wide shipping not shown yet?

Alcohol-based fragrance is regulated as a flammable liquid in transport. The production store will enable broader shipping only through a compliant ground-carrier workflow.

Can I return a decant?

The intended launch policy is final sale on filled-to-order decants for scent preference, with separate remedies for leakage, damage, incorrect items or fulfillment error.
