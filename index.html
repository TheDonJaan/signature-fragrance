SIGNATURE FRAGRANCE

Wear more. Commit later.

Signature Fragrance is built around a simple idea: full bottles are expensive, collections grow quickly, and a great fragrance is easier to judge after real wear than after one strip in a store. The shop turns a curated wardrobe into smaller, practical rotations so customers can discover what actually deserves a place on their shelf.

GOOD TO KNOW

Are these original fragrances?

Yes. The concept is to sell authentic fragrance transferred from original bottles into smaller atomizers. Signature Fragrance is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the fragrance houses shown.

Why are some scents more expensive?

Replacement cost varies dramatically. The prototype uses Core, Select, Premium and Reserve tiers so expensive niche and luxury bottles can remain available without hiding their cost inside every other product.

Why is national shipping not a launch promise yet?

Alcohol-based fragrance is regulated in transport. The prototype prioritizes GTA pickup and local delivery while a compliant dangerous-goods carrier workflow is finalized.

Do I need an account?

No account is required in this prototype. The intended Shopify storefront can remain guest-first for one-time purchases. Subscription management may rely on Shopify's secure customer/subscription management tools when live.

Can I return a decant?

The intended launch policy is final sale on filled-to-order decants except for leakage, damage or fulfillment error. Final Ontario-compliant policy wording will be reviewed before real payments are enabled.
